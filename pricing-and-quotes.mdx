## How the quote is built

The price is calculated from three things:

1. **Model and variant** - storage size, RAM, year.
2. **Condition** - your answers about screen, body, battery and functionality.
3. **Accessories** - box, charger and bill can add value.

The quote appears instantly and is valid when you book the pickup.

## Locked pricing

The price you accept at booking is the price you are paid, as long as the device matches the condition you described. There is no bargaining at the doorstep and no last-minute deductions. If the device differs from the description, the partner re-quotes transparently on the spot and you are free to decline.

## No fees

There are no listing fees, commissions or handling charges. Pickup is free. The quoted amount is the full amount you receive.

## Tips for the best price

- Answer condition questions accurately - honest answers keep the quote locked.
- Include the original box, charger and bill when you have them.
- Sell sooner rather than later: device values fall month by month.
