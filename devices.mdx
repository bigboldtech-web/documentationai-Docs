Cashkr supports 5,000+ device models across these categories.

## Categories and brands

- **Smartphones** - Apple, Samsung, Google, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Vivo, OPPO, Realme
- **Tablets** - iPad, Samsung Galaxy Tab, OnePlus Pad, Lenovo Tab
- **Laptops** - Dell, HP, Lenovo, Asus, Acer, MSI
- **Macs** - MacBook Pro, MacBook Air, Mac Mini, iMac
- **Smartwatches** - Apple Watch, Samsung Galaxy Watch, Google Pixel Watch, OnePlus
- **Cameras** - Canon, Nikon, Sony, GoPro, DSLRs and mirrorless
- **Headphones** - AirPods, Bose, Marshall, Nothing, Samsung
- **Gaming consoles** - PlayStation, Xbox

## Any condition means any condition

Working devices fetch the best prices, but Cashkr also buys devices that are broken, cracked, water-damaged or not switching on. Describe the condition honestly in the quote flow and the price you see is the price you get.

If your exact model is not listed in the app, reach out through the Help section and we will tell you whether we can take it.
