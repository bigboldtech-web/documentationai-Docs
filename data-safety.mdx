A factory reset alone does not destroy data - traces can be recovered with the right tools. Cashkr runs a **certified multi-pass data erasure** on every device it buys, overwriting storage so personal data is unrecoverable.

## What you should still do yourself

The wipe is our job, but before pickup you should:

1. **Back up** everything you want to keep.
2. **Sign out** of iCloud / Google / Microsoft accounts and disable Find My or activation locks.
3. **Remove** your SIM card and memory card.
4. **Log out** of banking and payment apps as a matter of habit.

## Why it is safe to sell a broken device

Even devices that do not switch on go through the same erasure process before resale or recycling - storage is wiped or destroyed. Your data does not travel with the device.
