## The pickup

- Pickups are **free**, at the address and time slot you chose.
- Every pickup partner is **background-verified**.
- The partner inspects the device on the spot against your quote answers: power-on, screen, body, basic functions.

## The payment

- When the device matches the description, payment is released the **same day**.
- Choose **UPI**, **bank transfer** or **cash**.
- Every sale comes with a **valid invoice** for your records.

## Before the partner arrives

1. Back up anything you want to keep - photos, chats, documents.
2. Sign out of accounts: iCloud and Find My on Apple devices, Google account on Android, Microsoft account on laptops.
3. Remove the SIM and any memory card.
4. Keep the box, charger and bill together if you included them in the quote.

Signing out of Find My or Google accounts matters: an account-locked device cannot be verified or resold, and the pickup cannot complete until the lock is removed.
