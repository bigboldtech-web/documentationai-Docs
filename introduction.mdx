Cashkr is a device buyback platform. You tell us what you are selling, we quote a price instantly, a background-verified partner picks the device up from your doorstep for free, and you are paid the same day by UPI, bank transfer or cash.

Cashkr is operated by BigBold Technologies Private Limited and serves 2,000+ pincodes across India through the Cashkr app and cashkr.com.

## Why people sell to Cashkr

- **The quote is the price.** Pricing is locked when you book. No renegotiation at the door, no surprise deductions.
- **Zero effort.** Free doorstep pickup at a time you choose. No listing, no buyers to meet, no fees or commissions.
- **Same-day money.** Payment lands after on-the-spot verification, by UPI, bank transfer or cash.
- **Your data is gone for good.** Every device gets a certified multi-pass data wipe that goes beyond a factory reset.
- **Any condition.** Working, cracked, dead: it still has value. We buy broken and non-functional devices too.

## What we buy

Smartphones, tablets, laptops, Macs, smartwatches, cameras, headphones and gaming consoles. See [Devices we buy](/devices) for the full brand list.
