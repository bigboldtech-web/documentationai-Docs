## Overview

Projects group your people, tasks, and goals for easy management.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new project">
    From the dashboard, click `New Project`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter details">
    Name your project and set a description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Pick team members to join and set permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click `Create`. Your project is ready for tasks!
  </Step>
</Steps>
