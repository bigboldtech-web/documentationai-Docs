## How fast is the payment?

Same day. Once the pickup partner verifies the device at your doorstep, payment is released immediately.

## Which payment methods are available?

- UPI
- Bank transfer
- Cash at pickup

Pick your preferred method when booking or at handover.

## Do I get a receipt?

Yes - every transaction comes with a valid invoice.

## The amount differs from my quote. Why?

The quote is locked to the condition you described. If verification finds the device in a different condition, the partner re-quotes transparently before anything is handed over - you can accept the revised price or decline and keep the device. Nothing is deducted silently.
