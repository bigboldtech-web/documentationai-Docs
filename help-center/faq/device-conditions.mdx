When you request a quote, you answer questions about the device's state. Answer these as the device actually is.

## The condition areas that matter

- **Screen** - scratches, cracks, dead pixels, touch issues.
- **Body** - dents, bends, missing parts.
- **Battery** - health and whether it holds charge.
- **Functionality** - cameras, speakers, buttons, biometrics, network.
- **Accessories** - original box, charger, bill.

## Broken or dead devices

Cashkr buys devices that are cracked, water-damaged or not switching on. Choose the matching options in the quote flow - a broken device with an honest description sails through verification.

## Why honesty pays

The doorstep verification checks exactly what you answered. Matching answers = the locked price, paid same day. Mismatched answers = an on-the-spot re-quote you are free to decline.
