---
title: Notifications and reminders
description: What actually creates a notification, where they land, how reminders fire whether or not the app is open, and which emails WorkwrK really sends.
---

WorkwrK has two separate systems that people often confuse. **Notifications** are things the product tells you about because someone else acted. **Reminders** are alarms you set for yourself.

## What creates a notification

These are the events that write a notification today:

| Event | Who gets it |
| --- | --- |
| A task is created for you, or reassigned to you | The new assignee |
| A task you are assigned changes status | The assignee, plus everyone who has commented on it |
| Someone mentions you in a task comment or a doc | The person mentioned |
| Someone comments on a task assigned to you | The assignee |
| An SOP is assigned to you | Each assignee. The notification links to `My SOPs` |
| A goal is created with you as its owner | The owner, when someone else created it |
| A teammate gives you kudos | The recipient |
| One of your reminders fires | You |

Two rules apply to all of them: you are never notified about your own action, and your own preferences are checked before the notification is written.

<Callout kind="alert">
  A task's **due date does not notify you on its own**. There is no automatic "due today" notification for tasks in Lists. If you need a nudge, set a reminder on the task, which is a separate thing described below.
</Callout>

## Where notifications land

- **The bell** in the top right shows recent notifications with an unread badge. Notifications can also be mirrored as desktop notifications, which you enable from the footer of the bell popover because a browser only grants that permission from a real click.
- **The Inbox** at `Inbox` in the Home sidebar is the full list. It has four tabs: `Primary` for mentions, task assignments and approvals; `Other` for kudos, due dates, surveys and SOPs; `Later` for anything you snoozed; and `Cleared` for what you have already read.

## Reminders

A reminder is an alarm with a time. There are two ways to set one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="A standalone reminder" icon="alarm-clock">
    Use the `Reminder` quick tool in the top bar. Give it a title, then either pick a time or use the chips `In 1 hour`, `This evening` or `Tomorrow 9am`. Tick `Also email me` if you want an email as well as the in-app alert. Press `Set reminder`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A reminder on a task" icon="calendar-clock">
    Open the task and use its scheduling popover. Alongside `Date` and `Repeat` there is a `Reminder` tab, where reminders can be set relative to the due date or at a custom time. These are real reminders attached to the task, so firing one deep-links straight back to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the `Reminder` tool is not in your top bar, open your profile menu and pin it from the personal tools list.

### How a reminder fires

Two mechanisms cover the two cases, and they cannot double-fire because each due reminder is claimed exactly once:

- **While the app is open**, the browser checks for due reminders on load and then every 60 seconds.
- **While the app is closed**, a scheduled job on the server fires anything due, so the reminder is waiting in the bell when you come back.

A fired reminder appears as a persistent card in the bottom right. It stays until you act on it. Each card offers `Snooze` with `10 minutes`, `1 hour` and `Tomorrow 9am`, plus `Dismiss`, and `Open` when the reminder belongs to a task. With more than one card you also get `Dismiss all`.

The bell keeps the whole lifecycle: reminders that already fired and have not been acted on, then Overdue, Today and Upcoming, then a read-only history of recently dismissed ones from the last 14 days.

Opening a task from its reminder counts as acting on it, so the card disappears. If you want to look at the task but keep the alarm, snooze it first.

## Controlling what you get

Everything is per person, under `Settings`, then `Notifications`. Changes save automatically. There are three groups.

**Inbox** decides what lands in your Inbox and bell: `Task assigned to me`, `@Mentions`, `Comments on my tasks`, `Status changes on my tasks`, `Due-date reminders` and `Kudos & recognition`. Switching a row off stops that notification being created at all.

**Email, Task & activity** has a master switch called `Email notifications` that governs the rows under it. Today only two of those rows actually send: `Task assigned to me` and `Kudos & recognition`. The rest are marked `Soon`.

**Email, Workflow & HR** is independent of that master switch. `KRA & KPI updates`, `Review reminders` and `SOP updates` are live. `Daily digest` is marked `Soon`.

<Callout kind="alert">
  A row marked `Soon` is not a toggle you forgot to switch on. It means WorkwrK does not send that email yet, so it is shown honestly instead of as a control that would do nothing. There is no daily or weekly digest email today.
</Callout>

## When you expected a notification and got nothing

1. Check the matching row under `Settings`, then `Notifications`. A switched-off Inbox row stops the notification being created at all.
2. Confirm you were not the one who acted. Your own changes never notify you.
3. For emails, check whether that row is marked `Soon`, and whether the `Email notifications` master switch is on. Workflow and HR emails ignore the master switch.
4. For a reminder, check the bell. A reminder that fired while you were away is not lost: it re-surfaces as a card the next time you open the app, and until you act on it the bell keeps it in its fired list.
