title: How do I invite my team to Workwrk?
description: Inviting colleagues to your Workwrk workspace.
---

## Overview

You can easily add team members to collaborate in Workwrk.

## Steps

1. Go to `Settings` → `Team` in the sidebar.
2. Click `Invite Member`.
3. Enter the email addresses and select roles.
4. Click `Send Invitation`.
5. Your colleagues will get an email to join your workspace.

## Tip

Resend invites anytime if needed from the Team page.
