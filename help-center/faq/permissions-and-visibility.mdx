---
title: Why can't I see this?
description: The three doors that decide who sees whose data, how Space and List visibility work, and why an app may be missing from your sidebar.
---

If something is missing in WorkwrK, it is almost always one of three rules: your access level, your position in the reporting tree, or the visibility set on a Space or List. Work through them in that order.

## The three doors

People data, KRAs, KPIs, goals and reviews all use the same ladder, and it is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface.

| Door | Who | What they can reach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `Employee`, `Agent` | Their own data only. Their career home at `Me` in the Home sidebar carries their KRAs, KPIs and goals. |
| 2 | `Manager`, `Team Lead` | Themselves plus everyone in their reporting tree, all the way down, not just direct reports. |
| 3 | `Director`, `VP`, `C-Level`, `HR`, `Company Admin`, `Super Admin` | The whole organisation. |

Two consequences people trip over:

- A manager with **no reports assigned** effectively sees only themselves. Reporting lines are set per person, so if nobody reports to you yet, the team surfaces will look empty. An admin fixes this under `Settings`, then `Reporting hierarchy`, or by setting a person's reporting manager.
- Door 2 is the *reporting* tree, not the department. Sharing a department with someone does not put them in your tree.

## Why an app is missing from the rail

Some apps in the left rail are gated by access level. They are hidden rather than shown as a locked door, and the pages behind them enforce the same rule if you reach them by URL.

| App | Minimum access |
| --- | --- |
| `Teams`, `Automation` | Manager tier: Team Lead, Manager, Director, VP, C-Level, HR, or an org admin |
| `Review cycles`, `Candor`, `Announce`, `Kudos`, `Surveys`, `Tools`, `Assets`, `Policies`, `Contracts`, `Trash` | HR or an org admin |

Everything else, including `Home`, `Planner`, `Docs`, `Library`, `Goals`, `SOPs`, `Timesheets`, `Forms`, `Clips` and `Dashboard`, is available to everyone.

The rail is also personal: apps can be pinned and unpinned. If an app you are entitled to has vanished, check the `More` tile in the rail before assuming it is a permission problem.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Employees are never stranded by the Teams gate. Opening a manager-only page sends you to your own profile, which carries your KRAs, KPIs, goals and reviews.
</Callout>

## Spaces

A Space carries one of three visibility settings, chosen in its `Share` dialog:

| Setting | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| `Private` | Only invited members |
| `Workspace` | Members, plus organisation admins |
| `Org-wide` | Every member of the organisation |

A Space appears in your sidebar if it is `Org-wide`, or if you are a member of it. Organisation admins see every Space. So a `Workspace` Space is still invisible to you until someone adds you as a member.

To be added, ask a Space owner or admin to open the Space's `…` menu, choose `Sharing & Permissions`, and add you. Roles inside a Space are `Owner`, `Admin`, `Member` and `Guest`.

## Lists and boards

A List has its own visibility, which defaults to following its Space:

| Setting | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| `Inherit Space` | Members of the parent Space. This is the default. |
| `Private` | Tighter than the Space: board members only |
| `Org-wide` | Looser than the Space: every member of the organisation |

Two extra rules worth knowing:

- A List inside a **private Folder** is hidden from everyone except the folder's owner, whatever the List's own visibility says.
- The Space **owner** can see through a `Private` List in their Space. A Space member cannot.

## Goals

Goals follow their own audience rules, which combine with the three doors:

- Company-level goals are visible to everyone in the organisation.
- Your own goals, and goals whose contributors resolve to you, are visible to you.
- A manager sees the goals of anyone in their report tree.
- Door 3 levels see everything.

Opening the URL of a goal you are not entitled to see returns a not-found page, not a redirect, so a missing goal and a forbidden goal look the same from outside. That is deliberate.

## Quick diagnosis

| Symptom | Most likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| A colleague's name is missing from a picker | They are not in your report tree, and you are not on Door 3 |
| A Space others talk about is not in my sidebar | It is `Private` or `Workspace` and you are not a member |
| A List inside a Space I can see is missing | The List is `Private`, or its Folder is private |
| A whole app is missing from the rail | It is access-gated, or you unpinned it. Check `More` |
| A team page redirects me to my profile | You are below manager tier |

Your own access level is shown as a read-only chip on your `Profile`, under `Settings` in the `Personal` section. If it says you should have access and you still do not, ask an organisation admin to check it under `Settings`, then `Members`, and your reporting manager under `Settings`, then `Reporting hierarchy`.

For the full model, see [Visibility and permissions](/reference/visibility-and-permissions).
