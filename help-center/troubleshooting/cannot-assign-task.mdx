---
title: I can't assign a task to someone
description: The four real reasons a person is missing from the assignee picker or the assignment is refused, and how to fix each one.
---

There are two different failures hiding behind "I can't assign this task": the person you want is not in the picker, or the picker works but the save is refused. Work through these in order.

## 1. The person is not in the picker

This is the most common cause, and it is not a bug. The people picker only offers people you are entitled to see.

- If you are an **Employee**, **Agent**, **Team Lead** or **Manager**, the picker offers you and everyone in your reporting tree, however deep. It does not offer the rest of the organisation.
- If you are a **Director**, **VP**, **C-Level**, **HR**, **Company Admin** or **Super Admin**, the picker offers everyone in the organisation.

**How to fix it.** Ask an organisation admin to set the person's reporting manager so they sit in your tree, under `Settings`, then `Reporting hierarchy`, or on the person's own record. If they genuinely should not report to you, hand the task to whoever manages them, or ask someone with organisation-wide access to make the assignment.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Typing in the picker searches the same scoped set, so searching harder will not surface someone outside your tree. If a name never appears no matter what you type, this is your answer.
</Callout>

## 2. You can only read the List, not edit it

Seeing a List is a lower bar than changing what is in it. Editing a task, including its assignee, requires edit rights on the List it lives in. You have them if you are:

- an organisation admin, or
- the owner of the List, or
- an `Owner` or `Admin` member of the parent Space, or
- for a `Private` List, an `Owner` or `Admin` member of that List.

Everyone else can open the List and read it, but a change is refused with a permission error.

**How to fix it.** Ask a Space owner or admin to open the Space's `…` menu, choose `Sharing & Permissions`, and raise your role to `Admin`. For a private List, the same applies from the List's own `Share` dialog.

## 3. You cannot see the List at all

If the task is not where a colleague says it is, you may not have access to its container:

- A `Private` Space shows only to invited members.
- A `Workspace` Space shows only to its members, plus organisation admins.
- A `Private` List inside a Space you can see is limited to that List's members, though the Space **owner** can still see it.
- Any List inside a **private Folder** is hidden from everyone except the folder's owner.

**How to fix it.** Ask to be added to the Space, or to the List. See [Why can't I see this?](/help-center/faq/permissions-and-visibility) for the full rules.

## 4. The task is archived

Archived tasks are excluded from a List's rows by default, so a task that "disappeared" has often been archived rather than deleted. You cannot assign what you cannot reach.

**How to fix it.** If you know the task's link, open it directly. Otherwise check `Trash`, where deleted items stay recoverable. Trash needs manager tier or above, and the left rail only surfaces it for HR and organisation admins, so a manager who cannot see the entry can still open it directly at `/trash`.

## Still stuck?

Collect these three facts before you ask your admin, because they are exactly what the admin needs:

1. Your access level. It is shown as a read-only chip on your own `Profile`, under `Settings` in the `Personal` section.
2. The Space and List the task lives in, and whether that List is `Private`.
3. Whether the person you are trying to assign reports to you, directly or indirectly.
