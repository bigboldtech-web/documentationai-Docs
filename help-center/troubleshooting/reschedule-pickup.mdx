---
title: Reschedule a pickup
description: Change the pickup slot or address for a booked sale.
---

Plans change - pickups can be moved.

## Change the time or date

Open your order in the Cashkr app and choose a new slot. Rescheduling is free.

## Change the address

Update the pickup address from the same order screen. If the new address is far from the original pincode, the app re-checks serviceability first.

## Cancel entirely

You can cancel any time before handover with no charge. Your quote may expire, so if you sell later a fresh quote is generated at current prices.

## The partner did not arrive

If a slot passes without a pickup, the order stays active - support reschedules it with priority. Reach us through the Support section in the app.
