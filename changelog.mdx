## Recent Updates

Workwrk releases updates regularly to enhance your experience managing people, processes, and performance. Check the [app changelog](https://app.workwrk.com/changelog) or enable notifications to stay informed.

<Callout kind="info">
  Updates deploy automatically. Restart the app or refresh your browser to access new features.
</Callout>

<Update label="2024-10-15" description="v4.0.0" tags={["feature", "breaking"]}>
## New Features

- **Marketing Relaunch**: Completely refreshed UI across all seven hubs (Home, People, Work, Money, Talent, Culture, Growth) with improved navigation and AI-powered insights.
- **Composite Scores**: View team performance at a glance with on-track, in-review, and at-risk indicators.
- **AI Cmd-K**: Natural language commands for quick actions like assigning tasks or sending kudos.

## Breaking Changes

- Updated API endpoints for performance hubs; migrate to new `/v4/performance` paths.

## Improvements

- Faster dashboard loading by `>50%`.
- Enhanced mobile support for all surfaces.
</Update>

<Update label="2024-09-20" description="v3.5.0" tags={["feature", "bugfix"]}>
## New Features

- **Kudos System**: Instant recognition across teams with emoji reactions.
- **Auto-task Assignment**: AI suggests and assigns tasks based on SOPs and KPIs.
- **Multi-country Support**: Localized for 8+ countries with currency conversion in Money hub.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed sync issues between People and Talent hubs.
- Resolved composite score calculation errors for large teams.
</Update>

<Update label="2024-08-10" description="v3.0.0" tags={["feature", "improvement"]}>
## New Features

- **Seven Hubs Launch**: Full integration of Home, Work, Money, Talent, Culture, and Growth surfaces.
- **Free Tier Expansion**: Unlimited use for up to 5 users, no credit card required.

## Improvements

- Shared data model connects all signals for holistic insights.
- Performance optimizations reduce tool sprawl from 15+ apps.
</Update>
