This page exists so you never open a WorkwrK surface expecting it to work and find out the hard way.

Three labels are used, and they mean exactly this:

- **Built**: you can use it end to end today. Verified in the code.
- **In progress**: the surface exists and part of it works. The specific gap is named. Do not rely on the missing half.
- **Coming soon**: the surface exists but there is no working backend behind it, or the backend exists with no way to reach it. Treat it as a placeholder.

Last verified against the codebase on 2026-08-18. Ordered by area.

## Settings

Settings is a two-door nav. The **Admin** door (org configuration) is hidden entirely for anyone below Company Admin or Super Admin, and its most sensitive pages (`/settings/identity`, `/settings/tags`, `/settings/structure`, `/settings/audit`, `/settings/data`, `/settings/defaults`) also enforce a server-side redirect so a manager cannot reach them by URL. The **Personal** door (`/account/*` plus Notifications) is available to everyone.

### Scheduled jobs

The time-driven jobs run from the production server's crontab, documented in `scripts/CRON-SETUP.md`. The ones whose absence used to be a caveat on other pages are registered: personal reminders every 5 minutes, recurring-task spawning hourly, goal check-in reminders on weekdays at 09:00, review-cycle auto-open daily at 08:00, KPI reminders on weekdays at 09:00, and the survey key rotation nightly.

Two things this does not cover, and they are the real gaps:

- The board-item due-date sweep has no job at all (see the row above).
- If you run WorkwrK somewhere other than the production box, none of these schedules come with it. `vercel.json` in the repository is reference-only.

## The legacy task table

Worth stating plainly because it explains several oddities above.

There are two task tables. Everything you create on a List is an `Item`. A separate legacy `Task` table still exists and is written only by `/api/tasks`.

Three places still read the legacy table and therefore cannot see your board work: `/analytics`, the performance score's task completion component, and `/api/tasks/workload`. The scheduled "tasks due today" digest is a fourth. Global search was another until it was rewritten over the `Item` graph. Migrating the rest is a tracked debt.
