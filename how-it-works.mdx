Selling a device to Cashkr is three steps.

## 1. Get an instant price estimate

Pick your device model and variant in the app or on cashkr.com, then answer a short set of condition questions honestly - screen, body, battery, functional issues, accessories. The quote appears instantly.

Answer accurately: the doorstep verification checks the same points, and an accurate quote is a locked quote.

## 2. Schedule a free doorstep pickup

Choose the date and time slot that suits you. Pickup is free everywhere we operate, across 2,000+ pincodes. A background-verified pickup partner comes to your address.

## 3. Get paid after verification

The pickup partner verifies the device on the spot against the answers you gave. When it matches, you are paid the quoted amount the same day, by UPI, bank transfer or cash - your choice. Every transaction comes with a valid invoice.

Before the device leaves our process, it receives a certified multi-pass data wipe. Read [Your data, wiped](/data-safety).
