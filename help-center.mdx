Most WorkwrK questions turn out to be one of three things: something is hidden because of your access level, something needs a permission you do not have, or a flow has a required step nobody mentioned. The pages below cover the real behaviour of the product, checked against what the app actually does.

## How to get help

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check whether it is a visibility rule" icon="eye">
    A surprising share of "it's broken" is really "you are not entitled to see it". [Why can't I see this?](/help-center/faq/permissions-and-visibility) explains the three doors WorkwrK uses and how Space and List visibility work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your workspace admin" icon="user-cog">
    Access levels, permissions, reporting lines, Space membership and invites are all set inside your own workspace, and your admins can change any of it without leaving the app. Your organisation admins are the people who hold the `Company Admin` or `Super Admin` access level; if you are not sure who that is, ask your manager. Admins find the controls under `Settings`, in the `Admin` section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Escalate account or billing questions" icon="mail">
    Anything your admin cannot fix from inside the workspace, such as a billing or account question, goes through the contact page at `https://workwrk.com/contact`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  There is no in-app support chat and no ticket queue inside WorkwrK. Your workspace admin is the first line for everything to do with access, membership and permissions.
</Callout>
